# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Refresh site marketing copy to be inclusive for youths missing the taste of home (while keeping the Kolkata homemade positioning) and update the flavor lineup shown in the Products/Our Flavors section.

**Planned changes:**
- Update all user-facing marketing copy to remove explicit “Indian Nepali youth/youths” mentions and replace with inclusive phrasing aimed at “youths missing the taste of home” (including hero text, Our Story highlights, and SEO meta description).
- Update the Products/Flavors data so the “Our Flavors” grid shows exactly: Dalle ko achar, aam ko achar, mula ko achar, bhaisi ko achar, chicken ko achar, and remove previous flavors.

**User-visible outcome:** Visitors see updated inclusive messaging across the site and a refreshed “Our Flavors” grid showing the new five-flavor lineup without layout issues.
